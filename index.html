The Journey

How I got here

I wasn't the strongest student growing up. It wasn't until class 11 and 12 that the real question started showing up — what did I actually want to do with my future? I finished school with good grades, but the bigger answer came right after: I wanted to learn to code. So I enrolled in a Python full-stack course while still working through college as a fresher.

Not having graduated yet made the job search harder than I expected, and that stretch without an offer was genuinely discouraging. What changed things was an internship — the first real chance to apply what I'd been learning. It rebuilt my confidence and gave me a foundation of practical skills I hadn't had before.

"One internship was all it took to turn uncertainty into momentum."

That momentum carried me into an OutSystems role — a platform I'd never even heard of before I joined. I learned it from the ground up, and three years later, it's the core of everything I build. Every project added something: a new certification, a harder problem, a bit more experience. Client work was the constant thread through all of it, and handling meetings became as routine as writing logic.

Somewhere along the way, that routine shifted into ownership. I started leading meetings instead of just attending them, coordinating teams instead of just being part of one, and shaping project requirements instead of receiving them secondhand. That's how I ended up here: an experienced OutSystems developer and technical project manager who shows up ready to give 100% to whatever the project needs.

"Ship what's needed, document what matters, mentor as you go." — My operating philosophy
